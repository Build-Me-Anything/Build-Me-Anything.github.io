Numerical evidence only. A converged dissipation peak says the *integral* energetics of this flow are captured; it says nothing about regularity. max|ω| is a local quantity — its level-to-level change is the number to watch, and the BKM integral ∫max|ω|dt, not the peak, is the quantity on which vorticity growth is judged.
